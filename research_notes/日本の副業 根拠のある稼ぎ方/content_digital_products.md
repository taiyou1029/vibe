## 1. note (有料記事 / メンバーシップ): exact fees and published creator-earnings data

### Takeaway
note takes a payment-method-dependent 事務手数料 (5% card / 7% PayPay / 15% carrier billing), then a 10% platform fee on the remainder (20% for 定期購読マガジン), plus ¥270 per payout. So a creator keeps about 76–86% of list price before the payout fee. note publishes no median or distribution, only top-1,000 averages (about ¥15.15M/yr) and platform totals. Earnings are highly concentrated and the typical creator's income is unknown.

### Cited Findings
- **事務手数料 (payment fee)** depends on the buyer's payment method and the creator cannot choose it: credit card 5%, PayPay 7%, carrier billing (携帯キャリア決済) 15%. — [note help: コンテンツを販売する際に引かれる手数料](https://www.help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/360011358873)
- **プラットフォーム利用料** is charged on (sale − 事務手数料): 10% for 有料記事, 有料マガジン, チップ and メンバーシップ, and 20% for 定期購読マガジン. — [note help](https://www.help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/360011358873)
- **振込手数料** is ¥270 per payout. Official example: a ¥1,000 article bought by card carries a ¥50 事務手数料 and a ¥95 platform fee, leaving ¥585 after the ¥270 payout fee if it is the only sale in that payout. — [note help](https://www.help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/360011358873)
- A third-party site claims card 5.0% and convenience-store payment 7.0% (税抜) after an "October 2025 revision". The search summarizer flagged that site as internally inconsistent, and the revision is unverified. — [app-tatsujin (third-party)](https://app-tatsujin.com/note-fee-structure-comparison-2025/)
- **Payout mechanics:** sales are confirmed (確定) on the 2nd of each month. A payout request made between the 2nd and the 20th is paid at the end of that month; a request made between the 21st and the 1st is paid at the end of the next month. The minimum request is ¥1,000 of unpaid sales. Confirmed sales have a 180-day 預かり期限 (holding limit), after which payment may not be possible. — [note help: 売上金を受け取る](https://www.help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/360010430873); [note help: 預かり期限](https://www.help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/900003987706)
- **Platform scale (FY2025, results published 2026-01-13):** 4Q GMV was ¥5,608M (+29.1% YoY). Registered members 11.14M, cumulative unique creators 2.02M (accounts that have posted at least once), published content 69.56M items. note pro ARR was ¥757M. — [note FY2025 決算説明資料](https://contents.xj-storage.jp/xcontents/AS05592/f0498e9d/b771/436b/8e46/d56515c4e881/140120260113532620.pdf); [investalk summary](https://investalk.jp/5243/note-2025-11-earnings/)
- **Concentration data (the only "distribution" note publishes):** the top 1,000 creators' average annual sales were about ¥15.15M for Dec 2024–Nov 2025, up from about ¥11.6M for Apr 2023–Mar 2024. Search summaries attribute these to note's official stats or IR material, but I could not open the primary. — [note FY2025 決算説明資料 (per search extract)](https://contents.xj-storage.jp/xcontents/AS05592/f0498e9d/b771/436b/8e46/d56515c4e881/140120260113532620.pdf); secondary: [assist-all](https://lifestyle.assist-all.co.jp/?p=54676), [hashout](https://hashout.jp/?p=12386)
- The same IR material reportedly says buyers return: over 90% bought articles in 2+ months of a year, and over 70% in 6+ months. — [note FY2025 決算説明資料 (per search extract)](https://contents.xj-storage.jp/xcontents/AS05592/f0498e9d/b771/436b/8e46/d56515c4e881/140120260113532620.pdf)
- No note filing found gives the number or share of creators earning ¥X/month or ¥X/year. — [search across note IR / JPX disclosures](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/52430/140120251007569452.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Effective take on a ¥1,000 sale, before the ¥270 payout fee** (my arithmetic from the official formula):
  - card: ¥855 (85.5%)
  - PayPay: ¥837 (83.7%)
  - carrier billing: ¥765 (76.5%)
  - 定期購読マガジン paid by card: ¥760 (76%)
- The ¥270 payout fee weighs heavily on small earners: on a ¥1,000 minimum payout it costs 27 percentage points.
- **Concentration:** the top 1,000 are about 0.05% of the 2.02M "creators". Their combined sales of about ¥15.15B are probably a very large share of annual GMV. The 4Q GMV alone was ¥5.6B, but I did not confirm the full-year GMV or that "sales" is defined the same way as GMV, so the exact share is not computable here. The point that holds: platform averages say nothing about the typical creator.
- **Startup cost** is essentially zero (free account). Time to first income depends on audience, not the platform. Once a sale happens, cash arrives at month-end at the earliest, subject to the ¥1,000 minimum.
- **Key risks:** no audience means no sales; platform fee changes; most of the 2.02M "creators" are likely non-sellers, which means low visibility for newcomers.

### Gaps
- Whether note premium or note pro changes these fees (not checked).
- Exact current fee for convenience-store or other payment methods, and whether an October 2025 revision really happened (only an unreliable third-party claim).
- Any official median, or count of creators earning more than ¥10k/month or ¥1M/year. Not found; probably never published.
- Full-year FY2025 GMV figure (needed to compute the top-1,000 share).

## 2. BOOTH, Brain, Gumroad: fees, and 特定商取引法 display for individual sellers (BOOTH and note)

### Takeaway
BOOTH's fee rose on 2025-10-28 from 5.6% + ¥22 to **5.6% + ¥45 per transaction**, and warehouse shipping became 2.5% of price + ¥25 per item. The ¥45 fixed part makes low-priced items expensive to sell. Gumroad charges 10% + $0.50 (30% for marketplace "Discover" sales) and has acted as Merchant of Record since 2025-01-01. No citable Brain fee data was found.

On 特商法: whoever counts as a 販売業者 must display the required details or state that they will disclose them without delay on request. **note** routes disclosure requests through note itself. On **BOOTH** the obligation stays with the shop owner, and no evidence was found of an intermediary disclosure mechanism.

### Cited Findings
- **BOOTH service fee from 2025-10-28:** 5.6% + ¥45 per transaction, up from 5.6% + ¥22 (+¥23 per transaction). The stated reasons were rising prices and higher costs from user growth. — [BOOTH announcement #834](https://booth.pm/announcements/834); [GameBusiness.jp, 2025-07-24](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2025/07/24/24711.html)
- **BOOTH 倉庫発送 (warehouse) fee:** changed from a fixed ¥26 to item price × 2.5% + quantity × ¥25, with fractions rounded up. Shops that had a warehouse order or storage application by 2025-07-31 got a grace period to 2025-12-02 13:00. The announcement is dated 2025-07-23 and was amended 2025-10-20. — [BOOTH announcement #834](https://booth.pm/announcements/834)
- **BOOTH 特商法 (official help):** BOOTH says sellers doing mail-order sales must show the legally required details in their shop's 「特定商取引法に基づく表記」, and that sellers who are not a 販売業者 have no obligation to list address or contact details. — [BOOTH help: 特定商取引法に基づく表記とは](https://booth.pixiv.help/hc/ja/articles/230689227)
- **BOOTH, no proxy disclosure (third-party):**
  - The default wording is 「請求により遅滞なく開示」 (will disclose without delay on request).
  - A 2017 anecdote reports BOOTH 事務局 telling a seller that the disclosure obligation lies with the shop owner.
  - Explainer sites say BOOTH has no mechanism to handle disclosure on the seller's behalf, so choosing to omit the details does not keep the address secret.
  - — [cremu.jp](https://cremu.jp/topics/1289); [bryog.com](https://bryog.com/booth-tokushoho/); [lomdesign.jp](https://lomdesign.jp/blog/booth-tokushoho/)
- **note 特商法 (official help):**
  - Display is required only for a 販売業者, meaning someone who trades 「業として」: for profit, repeatedly and continuously. Someone who is not a 販売業者 has no obligation to disclose.
  - A 販売業者 must in principle show address, name and phone number. They may omit these by stating that they will disclose them without delay on request.
  - On note, requesters send the request to note, not to the creator, and note does not hand creator information to requesters without the creator's consent.
  - — [note help: 特商法表示に基いて…掲載しなければなりませんか?](https://help-note.com/hc/ja/articles/360008947533)
- General explainers say individuals who sell by mail order continuously (not only companies) fall under the law. — [MoneyForward クラウド (2025-08-28)](https://biz.moneyforward.com/tax_return/basic/79606/)
- **Gumroad (third-party sources):**
  - Fees: 10% + $0.50 per transaction on direct and profile sales; 30% when the buyer comes through Gumroad's Discover marketplace; no monthly fee.
  - Gumroad became Merchant of Record on 2025-01-01, so it handles VAT/GST and sales tax.
  - Card processing (about 2.9% + $0.30 via Stripe) is said to be extra, giving an effective rate of about 12.9% + $0.80 on direct sales.
  - Fees are not refunded on refunds.
  - — [schoolmaker.com](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/gumroad-pricing); [checkoutpage.com](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees); [dodopayments (a competitor)](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained)
- **Brain (brain-market):** two searches found no citable fee, payout or affiliate-commission terms. — [search result set incl. atsoho comparison](https://atsoho.com/blog/note-brain-management-support-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- **BOOTH net on digital items** (my arithmetic, rounding rules not verified):
  - ¥300 item: fee ≈ ¥62, net ≈ ¥238 (≈79%)
  - ¥500 item: fee ≈ ¥73, net ≈ ¥427 (≈85%)
  - ¥1,000 item: fee ≈ ¥101, net ≈ ¥899 (≈90%)
  - Below about ¥500, BOOTH costs as much as or more than note's card-payment take.
- **Gumroad:** for a Japan-based seller, Merchant-of-Record status removes overseas consumption-tax registration work. But USD pricing, the fixed $0.50 + $0.30 per sale, and FX make low-priced items unattractive. All Gumroad figures are third-party and should be checked on gumroad.com.
- **特商法 risk:** if a seller is a 販売業者, BOOTH offers no shield for their home address. note's intermediary process gives somewhat more privacy, though the legal duty still exists. Whether a given hobby seller counts as a 販売業者 is a fact-specific legal question.
- **Startup cost:** BOOTH and note are free to open. Costs are production tools and time.

### Gaps
- BOOTH's payout (振込) fee, minimum payout and timing: not found.
- Whether BOOTH added any address-concealment or proxy-display option after 2023. Not found; third-party sources say none.
- Brain's platform fee, affiliate split and withdrawal fee: none found. Do not cite the commonly repeated "12%" without checking the official page.
- No sales or earnings distribution data was found for BOOTH, Brain or Gumroad sellers.

## 3. Kindle Direct Publishing (Japan): 35%/70% conditions, JPY price bands, KENP, AI rules

### Takeaway
On Amazon.co.jp, 70% royalty requires **KDP Select enrollment** and a list price of **¥250–¥1,650 (税込)** per the current official table. Many Japanese blogs still say ¥1,250, which looks out of date. The price must also be at least 20% below the print edition, public-domain works are 35% only, and delivery costs are deducted at 70%. KENP per-page rates are not officially published; third-party trackers put them around $0.0045–0.0048 per page. Since Sept 2023 KDP has required disclosure of AI-generated (not AI-assisted) content and caps uploads at **3 new titles per day**. There is no official income distribution.

### Cited Findings
- **70% on Amazon.co.jp:**
  - List price must be between ¥250 and ¥1,650 (税込) per the Japan pricing table.
  - Only KDP Select-enrolled eBooks qualify for 70% on sales to Japan (KDP lists the same rule for Brazil, Mexico and India).
  - If the JP price does not meet the 70% requirements, 35% applies.
  - The eBook price must be at least 20% below the Amazon list price of the print edition.
  - Works mostly made of public-domain material can only use 35%.
  - — [KDP help: 電子書籍の希望小売価格の要件](https://kdp.amazon.co.jp/ja_JP/help/topic/G200634560); [KDP help: Amazon.co.jp 向けの価格設定](https://kdp.amazon.co.jp/ja_JP/help/topic/G201849770); [KDP help: 電子書籍のロイヤリティ](https://kdp.amazon.co.jp/ja_JP/help/topic/G200644210)
- **Conflict on the upper limit:** many Japanese guides still state ¥250–¥1,250 for 70%. The official KDP table (JP and other language versions) shows ¥1,650. The date of the change was not found. — [reiwasyuppan.jp](https://reiwasyuppan.jp/kindle7035/); [mamou.biz](https://mamou.biz/kindle-publishing-royalty/); contradicted by [KDP help G201849770 (fr version also lists ¥250–¥1,650)](https://kdp.amazon.com/fr_FR/help/topic/G201849770)
- **35% band:** ¥99–¥20,000, and the book can also be sold in other stores. This is from third-party guides; official confirmation was not seen. — [mamou.biz](https://mamou.biz/kindle-royalty-structure/); [reiwasyuppan.jp](https://reiwasyuppan.jp/kindle7035/)
- **Delivery cost at 70%:** third-party guides say ¥1 per MB in Japan. One search extract of the English help page mentions a 10MB-related rule for Japan, but I could not read the page to confirm the exact wording. — [mamou.biz](https://mamou.biz/kindle-publishing-royalty/); [KDP help (en): eBook Royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210)
- **US change (2026):** on Amazon.com the 70% cap rose from $9.99 to $12.99 effective July 7. Existing titles stay at 35% unless the author opts in. The English help reportedly says similar changes apply to other marketplaces, but the JP table still showed ¥1,650. — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/?p=47035); [Indie Author Blueprint](https://indieauthorblueprint.substack.com/p/royalty-pricing-structure-changes); [KDP help (en)](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210)
- **KENP (Kindle Unlimited pages read):** Amazon announces a monthly KDP Select Global Fund but no headline per-page rate. One tracker reports $0.00482/page for April 2026 and a 2025 range of about $0.00452–$0.00465. No JPY per-page rate for Amazon.co.jp was found. — [profitable.app KENP tracker (third-party)](https://profitable.app/kdp/stats/kenp-rate); [Kindlepreneur KENP calculator (third-party)](https://kindlepreneur.com/kenp-calculator/); [KDP help: KENP](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G201541130)
- **AI disclosure (introduced Sept 2023):**
  - KDP's content guidelines require publishers to disclose AI-generated text, images or translations.
  - Content first created by an AI tool counts as "AI-generated" even if heavily edited afterwards.
  - Human-made content that was edited, refined or error-checked with AI is "AI-assisted" and needs no disclosure.
  - The publisher remains responsible for guideline and IP compliance.
  - — [Impress INTERNET Watch](https://internet.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/yajiuma/1533950.html); [ebookfriendly](https://ebookfriendly.com/amazon-requires-to-inform-of-the-use-of-ai-generated-content/)
  - Per a third-party guide, disclosure is made per title and per format, is not shown on the product page, and does not affect ranking or royalties. — [Scribecount (third-party)](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)
- **Daily publishing limit:** in Sept 2023 KDP confirmed a cap of 3 new titles per day, which may be adjusted. Affected publishers are notified and can request an exception. A blog says the cap still applied in early 2026, but no official 2026 confirmation was found. One source wrongly dates the cap to late 2024. — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [GIGAZINE 2023-09-22](https://www.gigazine.net/news/20230922-amazon-kindle-direct-publishing-limit/); [univers.studio (2026 blog)](https://univers.studio/blog/kdp-ai-content-policy-2026/)

### Inferences
- **70% requires Select exclusivity:** a JP author who wants 70% must keep the eBook exclusive to Amazon (Select). The 35% option allows wide distribution but nearly halves per-copy royalty.
- **The ¥1,650 cap** (vs the commonly cited ¥1,250) widens the 70% band for practical/business books. Writers should rely on the official table, not blogs.
- **AI-generated books:** they must be disclosed, and the 3-titles/day cap plus quality enforcement signal that mass-produced AI books are a target. Account termination is the tail risk (not quantified here).
- **Startup cost:** KDP has no listing fee in the official flow; cost is mainly cover design, editing and time. I did not cite this, so verify.

### Gaps
- No official or credible distribution of KDP author earnings in Japan (median, or share earning under ¥X). Amazon publishes none for Japan; none found.
- KDP Japan payment timing (commonly said to be about 60 days after month-end) and minimum payout: not verified in this session.
- JPY KENP rate history for Amazon.co.jp: not found.
- Exact date the JP 70% cap moved to ¥1,650, and whether the 2026 US change brings a further JP revision.

## 4. Blogging / affiliate: JAO「アフィリエイト市場調査」income distribution and the 2023 ステマ規制

### Takeaway
This is the only channel in scope with a recent, survey-based income distribution. In JAO's **アフィリエイト市場調査2025** (1,000 site operators, fielded 2025-12-01 to 12-04), **52.5% earned under ¥1,000/month**, of which 27.8% earned ¥0, and only **12.4% earned ¥30,000+/month**. Since 2023-10-01, undisclosed paid promotion has been illegal under 景品表示法 (ステマ告示). The legal target is the advertiser, so affiliate content effectively has to be labelled 「広告」/「PR」.

### Cited Findings
- **JAO アフィリエイト市場調査2025:**
  - Respondents: 1,000 affiliate site operators (affiliates and bloggers), surveyed 2025-12-01 to 2025-12-04.
  - ¥30,000+/month: 12.4%, up 3.0 points from the 2023 survey.
  - Under ¥1,000/month: 52.5%, down 13.7 points from 2023.
  - The top bracket is "¥300,000+/month" and includes individuals and corporations making ¥1M+.
  - Several reposts date publication to 2026 (reportedly June 2026); I could not confirm the date on the JAO page.
  - — [JAO: アフィリエイト市場調査2025](https://www.japan-affiliate.org/news/survey2025/); [DreamNews press release](https://www.dreamnews.jp/press/0000353074); [宮崎日日新聞 repost](https://www.the-miyanichi.co.jp/pressrelease/dreamnews/862061.html)
- **Breakdown of the under-¥1,000 group** (third-party, consistent with JAO's total): ¥0 = 27.8% and ¥1–999 = 24.7%, which sum to 52.5%. The same article claims ¥300k+ earners run about 2× the sites and 3× the work hours (not verified against JAO). — [newstrendblog (Substack, secondary)](https://newstrendblog.substack.com/p/100010005253023ai); [libecity summary](https://library.libecity.com/articles/01KAZA5S67N855NNWHBDTY9YRH)
- **ステマ規制 legal basis and dates:** a designation notice (指定告示) under 景品表示法 Article 5(iii), published 2023-03-28 and in force from 2023-10-01. It targets 「一般消費者が事業者の表示であることを判別することが困難である表示」 (advertising that consumers cannot tell is the business's own). — [issoh.co.jp explainer](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/4794/); [ECのミカタ](https://ecnomikata.com/ecnews/40259/)
- **Who is liable:** the regulated party is the business that supplies the goods or services (the advertiser). Third parties such as influencers who were commissioned are not themselves the target. — [ECのミカタ](https://ecnomikata.com/ecnews/40259/)
- **Affiliate ads:** they generally count as 「事業者の表示」, so suspected stealth ads have to be fixed or removed. — [commercepick](https://www.commercepick.com/?p=34844)
- **Older posts:** pre-October-2023 posts still online after the effective date are also covered. — [adish press release 2023-09-28](https://www.adish.co.jp/adish/contents/uploads/2023/09/20230928_adish-press-release.pdf)
- **Enforcement:** violations lead to a 措置命令 (cease-and-desist order) and public naming of the advertiser, with penalties if the order is ignored. The first 措置命令 came in June 2024 (a clinic that had Google Maps reviews posted in exchange for discounts). — [NTT東日本 BizDrive](https://business.ntt-east.co.jp/bizdrive/column/post_188.html); [issoh.co.jp](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/4794/)
- **ASP notice:** A8.net issued a notice to advertisers in Aug 2023 ahead of the rule. I could not read its contents. — [A8.net notice PDF](https://support.a8.net/ec/news/202308_01.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Implied 2023 baseline** (subtracting JAO's deltas): about 66.2% earned under ¥1,000/month and about 9.4% earned ¥30,000+/month. The 2025 survey therefore shows an improvement, but the median affiliate still earns about ¥1,000/month or less. Since 52.5% are under ¥1,000, the median falls at or just above the ¥1,000 boundary.
- **Survey caveats:** it is self-reported. Sampling (likely an online panel of people who identify as affiliates) is not described in the extracts. It excludes people who quit, so the true distribution for beginners is probably worse.
- **What ステマ規制 means for an affiliate:** although liability sits with the advertiser, advertisers and ASPs pass the requirement on through program terms. An unlabelled affiliate post therefore risks program removal and unpaid commissions even though the affiliate is not the legal target.
- **Startup cost:** low (domain plus server, roughly a few thousand yen a month, not cited). Time to first income is typically long and highly skewed; nothing citable was found here.
- **Key risks:** search-algorithm changes, ASP or program termination, and ステマ compliance.

### Gaps
- The full JAO 2025 bracket table (shares at ¥10k, ¥50k, ¥100k, ¥200k and ¥300k+) and its sampling method: the JAO page could not be opened and the search budget ran out.
- Exact JAO publication date (reported as 2026).
- Any measured effect of ステマ規制 on affiliate income or disclosure rates: none found.
- Primary 消費者庁 pages (告示 text, 運用基準, Q&A on affiliate). The site was not reachable and the search budget ran out, so the legal points above rest on secondary explainers.

## 5. YouTube Partner Program: thresholds, revenue share, Shorts, July 2025 "inauthentic content" update

### Takeaway
Today in Japan, ad revenue requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. The lower tier (500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views) unlocks only fan funding and Shopping, not ads.

**From 2027-02-01 the ad-revenue bar for new applicants roughly doubles** to 8,000 qualified hours or 20M qualified Shorts views. Shorts ad revenue will also require a rolling 10M Shorts views per 90 days, even for existing partners.

Revenue share is 55% for long-form and 45% for Shorts (after music costs). In July 2025 the "repetitious content" policy was renamed "inauthentic content", aimed at mass-produced, template-like uploads (including low-effort AI). YouTube publishes no creator income distribution.

### Cited Findings
- **Current ad-revenue tier:** 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, or 10M valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days. — [Impress Watch (2023)](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1536367.html)
- **Lower tier in Japan (since Oct 2023):**
  - Requirements: 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months or 3M valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days.
  - Unlocks fan funding only (Super Thanks, Super Chat, Super Stickers, channel memberships) plus some Shopping.
  - The current help still lists these criteria. Memberships also require the creator to be 18 or older.
  - — [Impress Watch (2023-10)](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1536367.html); [YouTube Help: YPP 概要と利用資格](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=ja); [YouTube Help: 拡充版 YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=ja&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- **Changes effective 2027-02-01 (official):**
  - New entrants for ad and Premium revenue sharing need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days, or 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days.
  - Requirements for fan funding and Shopping do not change.
  - Channels already in YPP keep their status.
  - All partners must accept the updated terms in YouTube Studio by 2027-01-31; partners who don't accept lose the related monetization features but stay in the program.
  - Shorts ad and Premium revenue requires a rolling 10M qualified Shorts views in the past 90 days, including for existing partners. Channels below that line can still earn from long-form, fan funding and Shopping.
  - New "active channel" tests: 1,000 qualified hours in 365 days, or 1M Shorts views in 90 days, or 2 long-form or 5 Shorts uploads per 90 days.
  - — [YouTube Help: YPP に関する変更](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=ja); [YouTube Japan Blog](https://blog.youtube/intl/ja-jp/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/); [CineD JP](https://www.cined.com/jp/?p=435719); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-partner-program-2027-changes/)
- **Revenue share:**
  - Long-form: creators get 55% of net ad revenue.
  - Shorts: creators get 45% of their allocated share of the Shorts creator pool, which is distributed by share of Shorts views.
  - Music costs come out before the pool is formed: no music sends all eligible revenue to the pool; 1 track sends 50% to music; 2 tracks send two-thirds to music.
  - Shorts ad revenue sharing started 2023-02-01, replacing the Shorts Fund.
  - — [Shopify blog (third-party)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization); [AIR Media-Tech (third-party)](https://air.io/en/youtube-glossary/what-is-youtube-shorts-monetization-shorts-fund-vs-ad-revenue-share)
- **July 15, 2025 policy update:**
  - "Repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content", with better detection of mass-produced or repetitive content that lacks originality.
  - Examples: narrative stories with only superficial differences, and slideshows that all use the same narration.
  - YouTube (Rene Ritchie) said the update does not target AI or reused content as such; reactions, reviews and clip-based videos with added value are unaffected.
  - Coverage differs on whether enforcement tightened; violations can mean removal from YPP.
  - — [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-targets-mass-produced-content-in-monetization-update/550337/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660); [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/youtube-issues-clarification-on-monetisation-policy-update-after-creator-backlash-145327.html); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155)
- **Only macro data for Japan (Oxford Economics, 2021):** YouTube's creative ecosystem contributed over ¥350B to Japan's GDP and over 100k full-time-equivalent jobs, based on a survey of 2,000+ creators. This is not an income distribution. — [YouTube Japan Blog: Impact Report 2021](https://blog.youtube/intl/ja-jp/news-and-events/2022_08_ytimpactreport2021/)

### Inferences
- **Deadline effect:** a Japanese newcomer who has not joined YPP before 2027-02-01 faces twice the watch-hour bar (8,000 h). Getting into YPP before then is therefore time-sensitive for anyone starting in 2026.
- **AI channels:** faceless, templated or AI-narrated channels are directly exposed to the "inauthentic content" rule. For a Shorts-heavy strategy, the 2027 rolling 10M-views-per-90-days requirement means many small Shorts channels will earn nothing from Shorts ads.
- **Startup cost:** low (phone plus free editing tools are possible). Time to first ad income is gated by the thresholds above. The lower tier gives a path to fan-funding income at 500 subscribers, but only if viewers actually pay.

### Gaps
- No official or credible distribution of YouTube creator earnings in Japan (median RPM, or share of channels earning ¥X). A planned search on YouTube Japan's "channels earning ¥1M+/yr" statistics could not run (budget exhausted).
- The 55%/45% split was confirmed only via third-party explainers in this session, not the YPP terms page.
- AdSense payment threshold and timing for Japan: not verified.

## 6. LINE Creators Market (stamps): revenue share and published earnings data

### Takeaway
Creators receive **50% of revenue after app-store fees, which is about 35% of the sale price** (since Feb 2015). That is roughly ¥42 per ¥120 stamp set, and the exact amount varies by purchase route. LINE has published only cumulative totals and top-10 averages. These show heavy concentration (in April 2021 the top 10 averaged ¥1.18B each), but there is no median or distribution.

### Cited Findings
- **Revenue share:** for stamps submitted from 2015-02-01, the creator share changed from 50% of sales to 50% of the amount after App Store/Google Play fees (about 30%), which is about 35% of gross. LINE said the change would fund more review staff and faster reviews. — [週刊アスキー](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/002/629/2629004/); [Yahoo!ニュース エキスパート (篠原修司)](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/c92237fc0912f6e2bd4368e5aaacaabf593053b8); [appllio (2014-12)](https://appllio.com/20141226-6044-line-creators-market-revenue-change)
- **Worked example (2026 explainer):** a ¥120 set loses about ¥36 in fees, and the remaining ¥84 is split, giving about ¥42 to the creator. The creator's share differs by purchase route. — [MoneyForward クラウド](https://biz.moneyforward.com/tax_return/basic/82130/)
- **Published stats:**
  - 2014, first month: top-10 average sales ¥4.7M.
  - 2015, first year: top-10 average ¥109M; about 393k registered creators in 156 countries; total sales about ¥8.946B.
  - 2018, 4th year: 1.5M creators; top-10 average cumulative sales ¥641M; total ¥53B+. LINE noted that these figures convert downloads into yen and differ from actual sales.
  - Apr 2021: cumulative total ¥106B; top-10 average ¥1,182.62M.
  - 2024, 10th anniversary: over 23M stamp packages created in 10 years.
  - — [appllio 2014](https://appllio.com/20140611-5341-line-creators-market-stamp-revenue); [appllio 2015](https://appllio.com/20150518-6462-line-creators-stamp-market-1year); [LINE press 2018 (PDF)](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/news/archive/L/ja/ja20181011_A.pdf); [LINE press 2021 (PDF)](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/news/archive/L/ja/ja20210506_A.pdf); [LINEヤフー 10th anniversary release](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/ja/news/release/009417/)

### Inferences
- **Top-10 share:** in Apr 2021 the top 10 creators accounted for about ¥11.8B of the ¥106B cumulative total, about 11% (10 × ¥1.18B).
- **Rough average per package:** dividing the 2021 cumulative total (¥106B) by the 2024 package count (23M+) gives about ¥4,600 gross per package lifetime, or about ¥1,600 to the creator at 35%. The years don't match and the mean is inflated by top sellers, so treat it as an indicative upper-side figure. The typical package likely earns far less.
- **Data age:** all LINE stats are 2014–2021 (2024 gives only a package count), so they are stale.
- **Startup cost:** near zero (drawing app). Time to first income is short once a set passes review, but sales depend on an existing audience.
- **Key risk:** massive supply (23M+ packages) means discoverability is the bottleneck.

### Gaps
- No distribution or median of creator earnings was ever found from LINE.
- Current minimum payout, payout fee and timing: not verified.
- How LINEスタンプ プレミアム (subscription) usage is paid to creators: not found.
- Post-2021 sales totals: not found.

## 7. Stock photo / illustration: PIXTA and Adobe Stock royalties, and generative-AI rules

### Takeaway
- **PIXTA:** pays 22–42% for non-exclusive contributors (rank by sales in the past year), up to 53% for exclusives and 58% for exclusive people-photo contributors. Subscription downloads pay fixed credits (about 0.27–0.85 credits). **PIXTA stopped accepting fully AI-generated works on 2026-04-20 and ended their sale on 2026-05-22.**
- **Adobe Stock:** pays 33% on photos, vectors and illustrations and 35% on video, with a $25 minimum payout. It accepts generative-AI content with mandatory labelling and strict naming and IP rules, and has had weekly submission limits since May 2025.
- **Neither platform** publishes a contributor earnings distribution.

### Cited Findings
- **PIXTA rank-based commission:** ranks are recalculated monthly from the past year's sales.
  - Non-exclusive (一般): 22% at 0–149 sales/year, rising to 42% at 5,000+.
  - Exclusive-item setting (独占販売): 30–48%.
  - Exclusive contributors (専属): 30–53%.
  - Exclusive people-photo contributors (人物専属): 42–58%.
  - Some of these figures date from 2019, and PIXTA's own guide pages don't all match each other.
  - — [PIXTA guide: クリエイターランク](https://pixta.jp/guide/?p=3340); [PIXTA guide: ランク制度変更](https://pixta.jp/guide/?p=14862); [DIME (2019)](https://dime.jp/genre/669386/)
- **PIXTA subscription (定額制):** plans of 10 downloads/month or fewer pay the single-purchase percentages. Plans of 11+ downloads/month pay per download: 一般 0.27–0.47 credits, 専属 0.35–0.65, 人物専属 0.40–0.85. The 11+ rates were revised in Mar 2023. — [PIXTA guide: 画像定額制](https://pixta.jp/guide/?p=14365); [PIXTA guide: 獲得クレジット](https://pixta.jp/guide/?p=3336)
- **PIXTA exclusivity eligibility:** sell only on PIXTA, with 150+ sales in the past year (rank 2+) or 50+ in the last 3 months. — [PIXTA guide: 専属クリエイター](https://pixta.jp/guide/?p=3081)
- **PIXTA credit-to-yen rate:** contributors without an invoice registration number get 1 credit = ¥105 (税込) for sales from 2026-10-01 to 2029-09-30. This is linked to the インボイス transitional measures. — [PIXTA guide: 定額制獲得クレジット見直し・インボイス対応](https://pixta.jp/guide/?p=68288)
- **PIXTA ends AI-generated material:** new AI-generated submissions stopped 2026-04-20 and sales of items handled as 「AI生成素材」 ended 2026-05-22 (announced 2026-05-26). The stated reason was a flood of AI submissions in a short period that made it hard to match buyer needs. PIXTA still accepts limited auxiliary AI use on the creator's own photos or works, as long as it doesn't significantly change them. — [PIXTA press release (PR TIMES)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000358.000008963.html); [Web担当者Forum 2026-05-28](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/n/2026/05/28/52719)
- **Adobe Stock royalties (official):**
  - 33% for photos, vectors and illustrations and 35% for video, on licenses under standard plans (subscriptions, credit packs, on-demand).
  - Custom plans pay the same percentage of the attributable amount, or a flat rate.
  - Large-subscription (350+/month) minimums per standard download, by lifetime licenses: $0.33 (0–999), $0.36 (1,000–9,999), $0.38 (10,000+).
  - Pro Edition pays 33% with no minimum.
  - — [Adobe help: royalty rates](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/royalties-pricing/royalty-rates-assets.html); [Adobe contributor royalties page](https://contributor.stock.adobe.com/royalties)
- **Adobe Stock payout:**
  - Minimum $25, and payout opens 45 days after the first sale.
  - Payment goes to PayPal, Payoneer or Skrill.
  - Without a valid tax form, up to 30% is withheld.
  - Temporary exception: contributors who received a Firefly bonus can cash out from $1 between 2026-09-17 and 2026-12-17.
  - — [Adobe help: getting paid](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/getting-paid.html)
- **Adobe Stock generative-AI rules:**
  - Gen-AI content must be marked as such at submission.
  - The contributor must hold all rights, and the AI tool's terms must allow commercial stock licensing.
  - Prompts, titles and keywords may not use names of artists, real known people, fictional characters or copyrighted works, and may not imply a real newsworthy event.
  - — [Adobe help: Submit generative AI content](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/submit-generative-ai-content.html); [Adobe help: Generative AI content](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/generative-ai-content.html)
- **Adobe Stock spam and limits:**
  - At most 3 iterations of similar assets are allowed; excessive similar submissions count as spam and can lead to account blocking or termination.
  - Contributors may not use multiple accounts to get around generative-AI upload limits.
  - Weekly submission limits were introduced on 2025-05-20. Only community-reported figures exist: tiers of about 200/500/1,000 per week and about 50 for new contributors. — [Adobe help: content upload guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/content-policies-guidelines/content-policies/content-upload-guidelines.html); [MicrostockGroup forum (community)](https://microstockgroup.com/fotolia-com/new-submission-limits-for-adobe-stock-contributors/)
- **PIXTA IR:** single-item creator payouts are computed by applying a set rate to sales. No contributor earnings distribution is disclosed. — [PIXTA IR (JPX, 2025)](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/34160/140120250310590854.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Per-download income is tiny:** an Adobe large-subscription download can pay as little as $0.33. PIXTA 11+ subscription downloads pay 0.27 credits for new non-exclusive contributors, which is about ¥28 at ¥105/credit. Meaningful income therefore needs portfolios of thousands of assets.
- **AI-generated stock:** as of 2026 it is effectively closed on PIXTA (Japan's main domestic agency) and rate-limited and policed on Adobe. AI-only stock is a shrinking channel for a Japan-based individual.
- **Startup cost:** low if one already owns a camera or illustration tools. Time to first payout is slow: Adobe's $25 minimum plus the 45-day wait; PIXTA payout rules not verified.

### Gaps
- PIXTA's minimum payout (換金) amount, fees and timing, and whether the 2019 rank percentages are still exactly current: the guide pages are inconsistent and were not opened.
- No contributor earnings distribution from PIXTA or Adobe.
- Official current Adobe weekly submission-limit numbers: only community-reported figures exist.

## 8. Which channels have reliable income-distribution data, and which don't

Only **affiliate/blogging** has a recent, citable income distribution (JAO 2025: 52.5% under ¥1,000/month, 12.4% ¥30,000+).
