# Low-barrier, low-risk ways to earn or keep more money in Japan (スキマバイト / フリマ / ポイ活 / ふるさと納税・NISA・iDeCo)

> Source-access note (read first): the session's egress proxy blocked direct fetches of nta.go.jp, mhlw.go.jp, kokusen.go.jp, www2.jpx.co.jp (TSE disclosures), help.jp.mercari.com and rodo.co.jp. Findings below come from search-engine extracts of those pages plus secondary sources (trade press, municipal pages, broker pages, tax-accountant Q&A). Where the cited URL is a primary page, the figure was read from its search extract, not from the full page. Figures that only came from secondary sources are labelled "(secondary)". Research date: 2026-10-25.

## Q1. スキマバイト apps (タイミー, メルカリ ハロ, シェアフル): scale, wages, how pay works, fees

### Takeaway
Spot-work apps are large and still growing: タイミー reports more than 14.2 million cumulative registered workers (April 2026), and パーソル総合研究所 estimates 4.52 million people actually did spot work. Average advertised hourly pay in 2026 is about ¥1,240 (ツナグ働き方研究所), which is close to ordinary part-time pay and not a premium. The client pays about a 30% fee on top of wages. Whether workers pay any payout fee could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
**Scale: タイミー (TSE Growth, 215A)**
- Registered workers: 6.67 million (Dec 2023) → 10 million (Dec 2024), about 1.5x in a year — [JPX disclosure 2025-01-10](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/215A0/140120250110549114.pdf); [LOGISTICS TODAY](https://www.logi-today.com/709696) (secondary)
- 12.74 million registered workers and 417,000 registered client sites as of end-Oct 2025 — [ONE CAREER company page](https://onecareer.jp/companies/90576) (secondary). FISCO also gives 417,000 registered client sites and 226,000 active client accounts in FY2025 Q4 alone — [FISCO report](https://fisco.jp/cms/contents/data/6/4413/INFO_FILE_4413.pdf)
- "Over 14.2 million" cumulative registered workers and 465,000 client sites (April 2026) — [Monex Scouter](https://scouter.monex.co.jp/report/zaimu/215A) (secondary; not confirmed against the 決算短信)
- FY ended Oct 2025: revenue ¥34.289bn (+27.6% YoY), operating profit ¥6.747bn (+58.9%). The report links the growth to severe labour shortages — [FISCO report](https://fisco.jp/cms/contents/data/6/4413/INFO_FILE_4413.pdf)
- After a change of fiscal year, "FY Apr-2026" covered six months: revenue ¥21,006m, operating profit ¥3,812m — [Monex Scouter](https://scouter.monex.co.jp/report/zaimu/215A); [Matsui](https://finance.matsui.co.jp/stock/215A/settlement/index) (secondary)
- Definitions in タイミー's IR: マッチング率 = 稼働人数 ÷ 募集人数. The amount paid to a worker = 給与 + 交通費 — [JPX Q2 FY2025 presentation](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/215A0/140120250612588059.pdf)

**Fee structure / payout**
- Client-side fee: in principle about 30% of the total of the worker's wages and transport costs (成果報酬型). Posting has no initial or listing fee — [JPX disclosure (2026-06-11 business plan)](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/215A0/140120260611568108.pdf); [kitaishihon](https://kitaishihon.com/company/215A/business) (secondary)
- Pay accrues to an in-app wallet, and the worker requests a bank transfer. タイミー partnered with セブン銀行 in 2019 to enable instant (即時) payment 24/365 — [PaymentNavi](https://paymentnavi.com/?p=84228) (secondary)
- Employment form: since 2022 all タイミー work is direct employment (給与所得), and the 業務委託 form was abolished. A 源泉徴収票 is issued inside the app — [MONEYIZM](https://www.all-senmonka.jp/moneyizm/74941) (secondary)

**Scale: other apps and the market**
- メルカリ ハロ (launched March 2024) registrants: over 5 million (end-May 2024) → over 8 million (announced 2024-10-23, with 120,000 cumulative stores) → over 10 million (around Feb 2025) — [ECのミカタ](https://ecnomikata.com/ecnews/ecmall/43265/); [Business Insider Japan](https://www.businessinsider.jp/article/295746/); [CommercePick](https://www.commercepick.com/?p=61873)
- パーソル総合研究所 (Feb 2025) estimates a "spot-work population" of 4.52 million, plus a potential 14.31 million. Basis: 85,455 respondents aged 15–69, of whom 6.5% had done spot work in the last 3 years — [労働新聞](https://www.rodo.co.jp/news/191028/)
- スポットワーク協会 member-app registrations: about 9.9 million (end-Mar 2023) and about 25 million (2024-09-01). These are registrations summed across apps (with duplicates), not unique workers — [PRESIDENT Online](https://president.jp/articles/-/89348)
- 日本人材派遣協会 survey: 45% had spot-work experience, and over half did it for extra income — [労働新聞](https://www.rodo.co.jp/news/190101/)

**Wages (market-level, ツナグ働き方研究所 monthly "スポットワークマーケットデータレポート")**
- Feb 2025: average hourly wage ¥1,302, the first time above ¥1,300 — [LOGISTICS TODAY](https://www.logi-today.com/881077)
- Sep 2025: ¥1,312 (+¥136 YoY); job-openings ratio 3.50. By job type, 倉庫内・軽作業 ¥1,305 and 運送・ドライバー ¥1,279 — [LOGISTICS TODAY](https://www.logi-today.com/881077)
- Oct 2025: ¥1,324, which was ¥22 above the ordinary part-time average of ¥1,302 — [LOGISTICS TODAY](https://www.logi-today.com/898155)
- Feb 2026: ¥1,237, which was ¥86 below the ordinary part-time average; openings ratio 3.26 — [PR TIMES (ツナグ)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000475.000004558.html)
- Mar 2026 ¥1,247. Apr 2026 ¥1,247 (−¥62 YoY). May 2026 ¥1,239, with openings ratio 4.36 — [PR TIMES (ツナグ)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000489.000004558.html); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000486.000004558.html)

### Inferences
- In 2026 the average posted spot-work wage (about ¥1,240) is only about ¥120 above the 2025 national weighted minimum wage of ¥1,121 (see Q2), and it fell below ordinary part-time pay. A 4-hour shift therefore grosses roughly ¥5,000 before transport. The value of spot work is flexibility and fast payout, not higher pay.
- Registered-worker counts (タイミー 14.2m, ハロ 10m+) heavily overstate active users. The パーソル estimate (4.52m who actually worked) is the better measure of scale.
- The 30% fee is charged to the client on top of wages, so the posted wage is what the worker earns before tax.

### Gaps
- Worker-side payout fees for タイミー (whether a transfer fee applies, and when) could not be confirmed from an official help page. Search results did not settle it.
- No company-disclosed average hourly wage or average monthly earnings per worker for タイミー was found. IR materials use GMV (wages + transport) and do not give per-worker averages.
- No usable registrant or wage data was found for シェアフル (owned by パーソルHD).
- メルカリ ハロ figures for 2026 were not found. The latest confirmed figure is "over 10 million" (around Feb 2025).
- Exact active-worker (稼働ワーカー) counts for タイミー were not obtained.

## Q2. Labour-law protections in spot work (労働基準法, 最低賃金, MHLW 2025 guidance)

### Takeaway
Spot work through these apps is direct employment, so labour law applies: the minimum wage, 休業手当 and the rest. MHLW's guidance of July 2025 says that, absent a special agreement, the labour contract is formed when the worker applies. If the employer then cancels or sends the worker home early for its own reasons, it owes 休業手当. The national weighted minimum wage for FY2025 is ¥1,121, but prefectures put it into effect between October 2025 and March 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 2025年度 地域別最低賃金: national weighted average ¥1,121, up ¥66, the largest rise since the guideline system began. MHLW announced the 答申 totals on 2025-09-05. The guideline (目安) was ¥1,118 (+¥63), but 39 prefectures set rates above it — [JILPT ビジネス・レーバー・トレンド 2025年10月](https://www.jil.go.jp/kokunai/blt/backnumber/2025/10/special_01.html); [livedoor News](https://news.livedoor.com/article/detail/29550810/)
- All 47 prefectures are now ≥¥1,000. Highest is 東京 ¥1,226; lowest is 沖縄・高知・宮崎 ¥1,023 — [JILPT](https://www.jil.go.jp/kokunai/blt/backnumber/2025/10/special_01.html)
- Effective dates vary widely: earliest 栃木 2025-10-01, latest 秋田 2026-03-31, and 6 prefectures take effect in 2026 — [JILPT](https://www.jil.go.jp/kokunai/blt/backnumber/2025/10/special_01.html)
- Conflicting figure: one recruiting-media article states ¥1,211, which is likely a typo — [townwork magazine](https://townwork.net/magazine/?p=138495); contradicted by [JILPT](https://www.jil.go.jp/kokunai/blt/backnumber/2025/10/special_01.html)
- MHLW leaflet (July 2025, いわゆる「スポットワーク」の留意事項等): for first-come, no-interview app matching, "特段の合意がない限り、スポットワーカーが応募した時点で" the labour contract is formed. If the employer makes the worker take leave or finish early for its own reasons after formation, 休業手当 must be paid — [労働新聞 2025年7月](https://www.rodo.co.jp/news/202655/)
- Litigation context: a university student sued two restaurant operators, arguing that a last-minute cancellation after matching was an unlawful dismissal during a fixed-term contract — [労働新聞](https://www.rodo.co.jp/news/202655/); [マネーポスト](https://www.moneypost.jp/1282411)
- 連合 ran a "スポットワークに関する調査2025" (Dec 2024, internet survey, 1,000 spot workers) — [JILPT 国内労働情報 2025年4月](https://www.jil.go.jp/kokunai/blt/backnumber/2025/04/kokunai_04.html)

### Inferences
- Because pay is 給与 under a direct employment contract, the posted hourly rate must be at least the minimum wage of the prefecture where the work is done. That gives a hard floor of ¥1,023–¥1,226 depending on location and on whether the FY2025 rate is already in force.
- The July 2025 guidance makes it harder for an employer to cancel without pay, which lowers the risk for workers. Its exact wording should still be checked against the MHLW leaflet.

### Gaps
- The MHLW leaflet itself (mhlw.go.jp) could not be fetched, so only press summaries were used. Details such as the treatment of worker-side cancellations or penalties are unverified.
- No 国民生活センター warning specific to スキマバイト apps was found (see Q9).

## Q3. Tax on spot-work pay: withholding, the 20万円 rule, 住民税

### Takeaway
Spot-work pay is 給与所得 and is withheld under the 日額表 丙欄, which means no withholding below ¥9,300 per day. It is never year-end adjusted (年末調整). An employee whose main job is year-end adjusted can generally skip the income-tax return if non-adjusted 給与 plus other income is ≤¥200,000. That exemption does not cover 住民税: the worker must still declare to the municipality.

### Cited Findings
- NTA Q&A (based on law as of 2026-04-01): withholding for spot workers is the sum of the tax computed for each day's pay under the 給与所得の源泉徴収税額表（日額表）丙欄 — [国税庁 No.2514 Q&A](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/gensen/2514_qa.htm) (read via search extract)
- Under 丙欄, daily pay below ¥9,300 has zero withholding. 丙欄 applies to employment of 2 months or less with the same employer — [MoneyForward クラウド給与 解説](https://biz.moneyforward.com/payroll/basic/122249/) (secondary)
- Filing requirement for people paid from 2+ employers: a filing is required if all pay is subject to withholding and 年末調整されなかった給与の収入金額 + income other than 給与 or 退職 exceeds ¥200,000 — [泉佐野市 資料「給与所得者で確定申告が必要な方」](https://www.city.izumisano.lg.jp/material/files/group/36/R04kyuyo-kakuteishinkoku.pdf) (reflects 国税庁 No.1900)
- If the main job is year-end adjusted, a filing may be skipped when the non-adjusted 給与 is ≤¥200,000. If tax was withheld, a voluntary filing may get a refund — [税理士ドットコム Q&A](https://www.zeiri4.com/c_1032/c_1003/q_43894/) (secondary)
- 年末調整 can be done at only one employer per person (normally the main job) — [税理士ドットコム](https://www.zeiri4.com/c_5/q_2144/) (secondary)
- タイミー guidance articles: if non-year-end-adjusted タイミー income exceeds ¥200,000 a year, a 確定申告 is required — [MONEYIZM](https://www.all-senmonka.jp/moneyizm/74941) (secondary)
- 住民税: there is no ¥200,000 filing exemption for local tax. A 住民税 declaration to the municipality is needed even when an income-tax return is not. Filing an income-tax return also serves as the 住民税 declaration — [money-lifehack](https://money-lifehack.com/article/4963); [税理士ドットコム](https://www.zeiri4.com/c_5/q_34005/); [さいたま市 案内](https://www.city.saitama.lg.jp/001/153/004/002/001/002/p094059.html) (secondary/municipal)

### Inferences
- Because 丙欄 withholds nothing below ¥9,300 per day, a typical spot shift (about ¥5,000) has no tax withheld. A worker who must file (more than ¥200,000 non-adjusted) may therefore owe tax, not receive a refund.
- With タイミー, each client business is the legal employer, so pay from several shifts can come from many employers. All of it counts as non-年末調整 給与 for the ¥200,000 test.
- The ¥200,000 rule is an income-tax filing exemption only. It is not a tax exemption: the income remains taxable for 住民税, and the worker must declare it.

### Gaps
- NTA No.1900 (全文) and No.2520 could not be fetched. One known extra exemption is unverified this session: no filing is required if total 給与 minus certain deductions is ≤¥1.5m and other income is ≤¥200,000. The writer should verify it on [国税庁 No.1900](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1900.htm).
- The 令和7年度 tax reform (higher 基礎控除 and 給与所得控除 minimum, the "103万円の壁" change) was not researched here. It affects whether students or dependents doing spot work owe tax.
- A secondary claim that the 甲欄 withholding threshold rose to ¥105,000 per month from 2026 is unverified.

## Q4. フリマ (メルカリ): fees, payout, scale, かくれ資産 estimates

### Takeaway
メルカリ charges sellers 10% of the sale price plus ¥200 per bank payout, and listing is free. メルカリ and ニッセイ基礎研究所 estimate the value of unused household goods (かくれ資産) at about ¥90.5 trillion in 2025, or about ¥715,000 per person. This is a valuation at average Mercari prices, not a realistic cash-out amount.

### Cited Findings
**Fees and payout (official help pages)**
- Listing is free. When a sale completes, 10% of the sale price is deducted as 販売手数料 — [メルカリ ガイド「メルカリの手数料」](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/65/)
- Bank transfer fee: ¥200 per request, regardless of bank or amount — [メルカリ ガイド「振込申請とは」](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/100/); [振込申請方法](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/356/)
- お急ぎ振込 adds ¥200, so ¥400 in total — [aucfan](https://aucfan.com/times/archives/46731) (secondary); official page exists at [メルカリ ガイド「お急ぎ振込とは」](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/325/)
- 売上金 has a 180-day transfer-request deadline. After identity verification it becomes 残高, which has no expiry. If unused at the deadline, it is auto-transferred to the registered account minus ¥200, up to 2 times. If no account is registered, or the balance is ≤¥200, it lapses — [メルカリ ガイド「売上金の振込申請期限とは」](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/96/); [自動振込とは](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/480/)
- In 送料込み listings, the seller pays shipping out of the proceeds, and the 10% fee is calculated on the sale price including shipping — [aucfan](https://aucfan.com/times/archives/46731) (secondary)

**Scale**
- メルカリ FY Jun-2026: revenue ¥229.2bn (+19%), core operating profit ¥44.1bn (+60%), both records. Japan Marketplace GMV grew +15%, back to double-digit growth for the first time in 3 years — [AdverTimes 2026-08-06](https://www.advertimes.com/20260806/article553174/); [決算短信 (JPX)](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/43850/081220260804508114_tse-acedifsm-43850-20260805343850-ixbrl.htm)
- Japan Marketplace GMV was ¥329.1bn in FY2026 Q2 (+11% YoY) — [CommercePick](https://www.commercepick.com/?p=79971) (secondary)

**かくれ資産 (household unused goods)**
- 2025 survey (announced 2025-11-20), supervised by ニッセイ基礎研究所 上席研究員 久我尚子: total about ¥90兆5,352億, or about ¥715,000 per person. By age, the 60s average is ¥1,007,328, about twice the 20s average of ¥487,744 — [DX Magazine](https://dxmagazine.jp/news/2554ko16-2/)
- Category shares: ファッション 33.6%, ホビー・レジャー 22.2%, 書籍・音楽・ゲーム 21.2%. Items likely to be thrown away in the year-end clean-up are estimated at about ¥10 trillion, or about ¥89,000 per person — [DX Magazine](https://dxmagazine.jp/news/2554ko16-2/)
- Definition: unneeded items unused for 1+ year and kept at home for no reason, valued at メルカリ average transaction prices — [DX Magazine](https://dxmagazine.jp/news/2554ko16-2/)
- Earlier estimates: about ¥37兆177億 (2018, ニッセイ基礎研究所) and about ¥66兆6,772億 (2023) — [ニッセイ基礎研究所 2018](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/user/report/researchers_eye/2018/eye181107-1_1.pdf?site=nl); [Web担当者Forum 2023](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/n/2023/12/01/46076)

### Inferences
- Net proceeds per item = sale price × 0.90 − shipping (in 送料込み listings) − ¥200 per payout, spread across all items in the payout. Batching payouts, or spending the balance inside メルペイ, avoids repeated ¥200 fees.
- The jump from ¥37 trillion (2018) to ¥66.7 trillion (2023) to ¥90.5 trillion (2025) partly reflects changes in method and prices. It is a marketing estimate from a party with an interest in the result, so treat it as an upper bound on sellable value, not as expected income.

### Gaps
- Shipping rates (らくらくメルカリ便 / ゆうゆうメルカリ便 size tiers) were not retrieved.
- The share of users who sell, the number of sellers, and domestic MAU for FY2026 were not found in search extracts.
- No data was found on average sale price or time spent per listing.

## Q5. Tax and licensing when selling personal used goods (生活用動産, 古物商許可)

### Takeaway
Selling your own household goods (clothes, furniture, appliances) is in principle non-taxable as 生活用動産. The exceptions are precious metals, jewellery, art and antiques worth more than ¥300,000 per item or set (gold bullion and coins have no ¥300,000 threshold), which are taxable 譲渡所得 with a ¥500,000 special deduction. A 古物商許可 is not needed to sell your own used items. It is needed for buying in order to resell.

### Cited Findings
- 生活用動産 (家具・家電・衣類・食器 etc.) is excluded from 譲渡所得 under the Income Tax Act, so the gain is non-taxable. Exception: "貴金属、宝石、書画、こっとう等で1個又は1組の価額が30万円を超えるもの" is taxable — [健美家](https://www.kenbiya.com/ar/ns/tax/tax_measures/7638.html); [MONEYIZM](https://www.all-senmonka.jp/moneyizm/inheritance/315881/) (secondary; primary = [国税庁 No.3105](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/joto/3105.htm), not fetched)
- "1組" means items normally sold as a set. Selling many items at once does not make them a set — [税理士ドットコム](https://www.zeiri4.com/c_5/q_38830/) (secondary)
- Gold bullion and gold coins have no "over ¥300,000" condition — [鮎沢パートナーズ](https://ayusawa-partners.jp/column/kin-jikin-golf-joto) (secondary)
- If taxable as 譲渡所得, a ¥500,000 annual special deduction applies — [MONEYIZM](https://www.all-senmonka.jp/moneyizm/inheritance/315881/) (secondary)
- One tax accountant says NTA's view is that collectible figures, which are originally traded at high prices, are not 生活用動産. This is an individual answer, not a ruling — [税理士ドットコム](https://www.zeiri4.com/c_5/q_73569/) (secondary)
- 古物営業法: selling items you used yourself, or bought for your own use but did not use, is outside 古物営業. Items bought with intent to resell are not excluded — [新潟県警察 古物営業許可](https://www.pref.niigata.lg.jp/site/kenkei/tetuzuki-keibigyou-1-keibigyou-kobutu1-keibigyou-kobutu2-keibigyou-kobutu2.html)
- The criteria are profit motive, repetition or continuity, and purchase for resale. Buying for resale needs a licence even once. Unlicensed operation can be punished by up to 3 years' 拘禁刑 or a fine of up to ¥1m — [stockcrew](https://stockcrew.co.jp/insights/article_tenbai_sedori_legal_2026-06-11); [MONEYIZM / oikura](https://oikura.jp/shopsupport/guidebook/start/start2070) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Selling 不用品 you already own is about the lowest-risk option on tax and licensing. The risk rises if the seller (a) sells high-value jewellery, art or collectibles over ¥300,000, or (b) starts buying goods to resell (せどり), which can make the profit 事業所得 or 雑所得 and require a 古物商許可.

### Gaps
- The NTA pages (No.3105, No.3152) were not fetched directly. The exact wording on when repeated selling becomes 事業所得 or 雑所得 is not confirmed.
- The rules for platforms reporting seller data to tax authorities (e.g., the OECD model rules for platform operators) were not researched.

## Q6. ポイ活: realistic monthly gains and tax treatment

### Takeaway
Industry surveys from 2025 repeatedly find that about half of active point collectors earn ¥1,000 or more a month, so realistic gains are in the low thousands of yen per month. Points received as a discount on purchases are not taxed. Points won in lotteries or campaigns are 一時所得, which has a ¥500,000 annual special deduction. Since 2025-10-01, portals may no longer grant points on ふるさと納税 donations.

### Cited Findings
- GMOメディア survey (June 2025, n=5,468): about half of people who consciously collect points earn ≥¥1,000 per month. 98% use ポイ活 at least weekly — [BCN+R 2025-07-21](https://www.bcnretail.com/topics_detail9/20250721_538281.html); [GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2025/07/17/24659.html)
- フルーツメール survey (Jan 2025, n=1,000): about half earn ≥¥1,000 per month — [リセマム](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20250127/134004.html)
- ハピタス (オズビジョン) user survey (June 2025): average cumulative points about ¥75,000, median about ¥44,000, and about 1 in 4 at ¥100,000+ cumulative. These are cumulative over the user's history, not monthly figures — [GameBusiness.jp 2025-09-04](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2025/09/04/25008.html)
- DIGITALIO survey: over 30% earn points worth "年間◯万円" or more — [ECのミカタ](https://ecnomikata.com/ecnews/marketing/43631/) (headline only)
- Tax (NTA No.1907, per secondary reporting): points granted in proportion to purchases or payments and then used to pay are not a taxable economic benefit. Points won by chance (lottery campaigns) are 一時所得, computed as (income − expenses) − special deduction (up to ¥500,000) — [BCN+R 2020-01-16 on NTA guidance](https://www.bcnretail.com/topics_detail9/20200116_154522.html)
- Sign-up campaign points tend to be 一時所得. Survey rewards may be 一時所得 or 雑所得 (accountant view) — [CREX](https://crexgroup.com/ja/creditcard/knowledge/credit-card-points-tax/) (secondary)
- ふるさと納税 point ban: a 総務省 告示 of 2024-06-28 bans donations via portals that grant points, from October 2025. Ordinary credit-card payment points are still allowed. 楽天 filed suit, but the ban stood — [ファイナンシャルフィールド](https://financial-field.com/tax/entry-430168); [PaymentNavi](https://paymentnavi.com/paymentnews/165954.html)

### Inferences
- About ¥1,000+ per month for the typical engaged user means about ¥12,000+ a year. That is well within the ¥500,000 一時所得 deduction, so tax is unlikely to bite for ordinary users.
- All the ポイ活 surveys come from point-site operators or marketing firms with self-selected respondents, which likely biases them upward. No government statistic was found.

### Gaps
- No credible survey measuring time spent per month, so there is no hourly-equivalent figure.
- The NTA No.1907 page itself was not fetched.

## Q7. ふるさと納税: net-cost mechanism and scale (no new work required)

### Takeaway
Donations above ¥2,000, up to an income-dependent limit, are credited in principle in full against 所得税 and 住民税. The net cost is therefore ¥2,000, and the donor receives 返礼品. It does not raise income: it is a pre-payment of tax that is exchanged for goods. Donations reached a record ¥1.3314 trillion in FY2025.

### Cited Findings
- Mechanism: let A = donation − ¥2,000. The credit has three parts: 所得税 deduction, 住民税 基本分 (A × 10%), and 住民税 特例分 (A × (100% − 10% − the income-tax rate)). Up to the limit, the full donation above ¥2,000 is credited — [浜松市 資料](https://www.city.hamamatsu.shizuoka.jp/documents/173197/zeikoujo.pdf); [ニッセイ基礎研究所](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/80399_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli)
- The 特例分 is capped at 20% of the 住民税所得割. The limit varies with income and family structure, and 総務省's portal publishes reference tables for salaried people without other deductions — [ニッセイ基礎研究所](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/64833_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli); [武蔵村山市](https://www.city.musashimurayama.lg.jp/kurashi/1011908/furusato/1000291.html)
- ワンストップ特例: salaried people who do not otherwise need to file, and who donate to 5 or fewer municipalities, can claim without a 確定申告 by sending the application to each recipient — [浜松市 資料](https://www.city.hamamatsu.shizuoka.jp/documents/173197/zeikoujo.pdf)
- FY2024 donations: ¥1兆2,728億 (record at the time) — [泉佐野市 2026-07-31 press release](https://www.city.izumisano.lg.jp/material/files/group/19/PR20260731-2.pdf); [山田&パートナーズ](https://www.yamada-partners.jp/tax-topics/r080803)
- FY2025 donations (総務省 現況調査, published 2026-07-31, based on projected settlement figures): ¥1兆3,314億 (+4.6%), 5,963万件 (+1.4%) — [山田&パートナーズ](https://www.yamada-partners.jp/tax-topics/r080803); [総務省 archive](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_sosiki/jichi_zeisei/czaisei/czaisei_seido/furusato/archive/other_archives.html)

### Inferences
- The household gain is the market value of 返礼品 minus ¥2,000. Donating above one's limit raises the net cost because the excess is not credited, so estimating the limit correctly is the main risk.

### Gaps
- Not verified this session: the 総務省 返礼品 rules (返礼品 at most 30% of the donation, total costs at most 50%) and any rule changes said to take effect in October 2026 (one secondary source mentioned a change).
- The 総務省 ふるさと納税ポータル pages could not be fetched directly.

## Q8. NISA / iDeCo tax benefits (factual, no advice)

### Takeaway
New NISA (from 2024) makes investment gains on up to ¥18m (lifetime limit) tax-free with no time limit, and the annual limit is ¥3.6m. iDeCo contribution caps rise in December 2026: to ¥62,000 per month for employees and ¥75,000 per month for 第1号. Both use market-priced products, so principal is not guaranteed.

### Cited Findings
- NISA annual limit ¥3.6m (つみたて投資枠 ¥1.2m + 成長投資枠 ¥2.4m). Lifetime 非課税保有限度額 ¥18m, of which at most ¥12m in 成長投資枠. Tax-free holding period has no limit. The limit used by sold holdings is restored the following year (at book value) — [松井証券](https://www.matsui.co.jp/nisa/about/); [楽天証券](https://www.rakuten-sec.co.jp/web/nisa/limit/)
- iDeCo reform: from December 2026, the 第2号 (employee) cap becomes ¥62,000 per month, combined with any company-pension contributions, and the 第1号 cap becomes ¥75,000. It applies from the January 2027 debit, and raising contributions requires a procedure. Eligibility extends to under age 70 (new 第5号加入者) — [厚生労働省 資料](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12500000/001726959.pdf); [マネックス証券 2026-05-14](https://info.monex.co.jp/news/2026/20260514_01.html); [松井証券 2026-07-31](https://www.matsui.co.jp/news/2026/detail_0731_01.html)
- Before the reform, the cap for employees without a company pension was ¥23,000 per month — [松井証券](https://www.matsui.co.jp/news/2026/detail_0731_01.html)

### Inferences
- NISA products are listed stocks and investment trusts, whose prices move, so losses are possible. The tax exemption only applies to gains. Source pages describe the eligible products as market-priced, but no source explicitly said "元本保証なし".

### Gaps
- Not directly verified this session: the iDeCo benefits (掛金全額所得控除; no withdrawal before age 60 in principle; account fees charged by 国民年金基金連合会 and the administrators) and the official FSA NISA page. These should be checked at the iDeCo公式サイト and 金融庁 before use.

## Q9. Risks: 国民生活センター warnings (副業詐欺, etc.)

### Takeaway
国民生活センター (the National Consumer Affairs Center) warns repeatedly about "easy money" side-job schemes such as simple tasks or "just press like". Consultations about simple-task side jobs rose in 2024. No 国民生活センター warning specific to legitimate spot-work apps was found; the main documented spot-work risk is last-minute cancellation, which the MHLW guidance addresses.

### Cited Findings
- Total consumer consultations in FY2025: 1.006 million, up from 913,000 in FY2024 (about +90,000). Consultations involving people aged 65+ were 330,497 — [国民生活センター 2026-08-05](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/news/data/n-20260805_1.html)
- Simple-task side jobs: 950 consultations to 2024-07-31, against 703 in the same period of 2023 — [INTERNET Watch](https://internet.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1621607.html)
- Warning content: ads that stress "簡単に稼げる" or "儲かる" may be fraud. Do not disclose personal data easily, and consult a 消費生活センター (188) if asked to pay — [国民生活センター](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/news/data/n-20260805_1.html) (via search extract); [INTERNET Watch](https://internet.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2110573.html)
- Related: 国民生活センター has also warned about young people being told by side-job or investment solicitors to borrow from several lenders — [国民生活センター 相談 annual report](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/soudan_topics/data/annualreport.html) (via search extract; date unconfirmed)
- Businesses claiming to "increase unemployment benefits": 216 consultations in FY2025 through October, 2.4x the prior year — [秋田魁新報 2025-12-06](https://www.sakigake.jp/news/article/20251206CO0008/)

### Inferences
- The options in this note have known operators and clear fee terms (direct-employment spot work, selling one's own goods on メルカリ, mainstream point programs, government schemes). Schemes that require an upfront payment or the purchase of an "information product" fall in the pattern 国民生活センター flags as high-risk.

### Gaps
- The FY2025 count of 副業・もうけ話 consultations was not found. 国民生活センター pages could not be fetched directly.
- No 国民生活センター notice on スキマバイト-app trouble was found. This may mean none exists, or the searches missed it.

## Q10. Realistic earnings per hour vs effort (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Only spot work has a measurable hourly return: about ¥1,240 per hour average in 2026, with a legal floor of ¥1,023–¥1,226 by prefecture. Selling your own goods is a one-off source of cash with zero tax on ordinary items. ポイ活 yields about ¥1,000+ per month for half of engaged users. ふるさと納税, NISA and iDeCo do not generate income but reduce tax.

### Cited Findings
- Spot work: average posted wage ¥1,237–¥1,247 (Feb–May 2026), below ordinary part-time pay in Feb 2026 — [PR TIMES (ツナグ)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000475.000004558.html); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000489.000004558.html). The minimum wage floor is ¥1,023–¥1,226 — [JILPT](https://www.jil.go.jp/kokunai/blt/backnumber/2025/10/special_01.html)
- Mercari: a 10% fee plus ¥200 per payout — [メルカリ ガイド](https://help.jp.mercari.com/guide/articles/65/). A theoretical stock of about ¥715,000 per person in かくれ資産, and about ¥89,000 per person in items likely to be discarded at year-end — [DX Magazine](https://dxmagazine.jp/news/2554ko16-2/)
- ポイ活: about half of engaged users earn ≥¥1,000 per month — [BCN+R](https://www.bcnretail.com/topics_detail9/20250721_538281.html); [リセマム](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20250127/134004.html)
- ふるさと納税: net cost ¥2,000 per year within the limit — [浜松市 資料](https://www.city.hamamatsu.shizuoka.jp/documents/173197/zeikoujo.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rough illustrative maths: 8 hours of spot work a month at about ¥1,240 ≈ ¥9,900 gross. That is 10x the "¥1,000 per month" ポイ活 benchmark but requires physical work. A year of such shifts (about ¥119,000) stays under the ¥200,000 income-tax filing threshold for someone whose main job is year-end adjusted, but still requires a 住民税 declaration.
- メルカリ income is front-loaded (clearing existing stock) and not recurring unless the seller moves into reselling, which brings 古物商 and tax obligations.

### Gaps
- No credible data was found on time per sale on メルカリ, minutes per day spent on ポイ活, or commuting time and cost for spot work. Hourly comparisons other than for spot work are therefore not evidence-based.
