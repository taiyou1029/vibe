# Side income from freelance software development and skill selling in Japan (副業フリーランス・スキル販売)

> Method note for the report writer: in this session WebFetch and curl were blocked by the egress proxy (DNS failure / CONNECT 403) for every target domain. So every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not a full read of the page. The extracts usually quoted the source text directly, and I cite the page the extract came from. Figures that came only from blogs or aggregators are flagged. ## 1. Official and large-survey data on freelancer and side-job income

### Takeaway
Every large survey finds that most people doing freelance work as a side job earn little from it. ランサーズ 2024: about 7 in 10 of all freelancers earned ≤¥99万 a year from freelancing, and 37.6% earned under ¥10万. 内閣官房 2020: "under ¥100万" was the most common bracket for side-job freelancers. パーソル 2025: the median side-job hourly pay is ¥2,083, against a mean of ¥3,617, so a few high earners pull the average up. Surveys built around established professionals (フリーランス白書) show much higher incomes, because most of their respondents freelance full time.

### Cited Findings
**ランサーズ「フリーランス実態調査 2024年」 (published 2025-03-24; the latest edition found; no "2025年版" was found)**
- Japan's freelance population in 2024 was 1,303万人, with an economic scale of ¥20兆3,200億, about 40% higher than ten years earlier. The population has fallen since the 2021 COVID-remote peak. — [ランサーズ press release](https://www.lancers.co.jp/news/pr/24055/); [Biz/Zine](https://bizzine.jp/article/detail/11359)
- Method: online survey run 2025-01-16 to 01-20 of people aged 20–69 who earned pay for freelance work in Jan–Dec 2024; 2,929 valid responses. — [ランサーズ research page](https://www.lancers.jp/research_news/2024); [@IT/ITmedia](https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/articles/2503/26/news082.html)
- Income distribution, as reported by ITmedia: 73.1% earned ≤¥99万 a year from freelancing and 37.6% earned under ¥10万. Only 32.0% said they were satisfied with their freelance income. — [@IT/ITmedia](https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/articles/2503/26/news082.html). Another write-up gives "年収99万円以下が約7割". — [Biz/Zine](https://bizzine.jp/article/detail/11359). (Verify: a later search could not reproduce the exact 73.1% figure. Treat "about 70%" as safe.)
- ランサーズ says 副業系 and 複業系 workers tend to have low freelance income, but often have stable income from their main job. 自由業系 and 自営業系 workers earn more. — [ランサーズ research page](https://www.lancers.jp/research_news/2024); [Biz/Zine](https://bizzine.jp/article/detail/11359)
- ランサーズ issued a correction notice for an earlier edition. It gives 副業系すきまワーカー as 467万人 and 複業系パラレルワーカー as 299万人; which edition this applies to was not clear from the extract. — [ランサーズ お詫び](https://www.lancers.co.jp/news/info/20563/)

**内閣官房「フリーランス実態調査」 (2020; the government's main population estimate)**
- Japan has an estimated 462万 freelancers including side-job freelancers: 214万 doing it as their main job and 248万 as a side job. So more than half of all freelancers are 副業. — [内閣官房 調査結果 (令和2年5月)](https://www.cas.go.jp/jp/seisaku/atarashii_sihonsyugi/freelance/dai1/siryou13.pdf)
- For main-job freelancers who are the household's main earner, the most common income bracket was ¥200万–300万 (19%). For side-job freelancers and non-main earners, the most common bracket was under ¥100万. There were 3,062 side-job respondents. "年収" here means sales minus expenses (所得), before tax and social insurance. Survey period 2020-02-10 to 03-06. — [内閣官房 siryou13.pdf](https://www.cas.go.jp/jp/seisaku/atarashii_sihonsyugi/freelance/dai1/siryou13.pdf); [参議院 答弁書](https://www.sangiin.go.jp/japanese/joho1/kousei/syuisyo/204/touh/t204102.htm)
- A later 令和4年度 survey, run jointly by 内閣官房, 公取委, 厚労省 and 中企庁, covered 2,119 sole proprietors with no employees, working as a main or side job; survey date 2022-08. — [令和4年度 調査概要](https://www.cas.go.jp/jp/seisaku/atarashii_sihonsyugi/freelance/dai1/siryou15.pdf)

**フリーランス協会「フリーランス白書2026」 (published 2026-06-09; survey run September 2025)**
- 49.5% of respondents earned ¥400万 or more a year, and 27.7% earned ¥200万–400万. 48.5% work 140 hours a month or more, which is full-time equivalent. — [フリーランス協会 blog release](https://blog.freelance-jp.org/20260609-25645/); [白書2026 PDF](https://blog.freelance-jp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/FreelanceSurvey2026-3.pdf)
- 白書2025 is reported as showing 47.7% at ¥400万 or more. This comes from a secondary blog citation; verify against the [白書2025 PDF](https://blog.freelance-jp.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FreelanceSurvey2025.pdf). — [atsoho blog (secondary)](https://atsoho.com/blog/freelance-nenshu-ranking)

**Surveys of side jobs in general (all side jobs, not only freelance)**
- パーソル総合研究所「第四回 副業の実態・意識に関する定量調査」 (published 2025-10-28; survey run 2025-08-01 to 08-07; 1,500 executives and HR staff plus 62,320 workers aged 20–69): side-job hourly pay averaged ¥3,617 with a median of ¥2,083, the highest since the survey began. People spent an average of 23.0 hours a month on side jobs, and younger people spent more. — [パーソル総研 release](https://rc.persol-group.co.jp/news/release-20251028-1000-1/); [release PDF](https://rc.persol-group.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/news-release-20251028-1000-1.pdf); [マイナビニュース](https://news.mynavi.jp/article/20251029-3600318/)
- JILPT 調査シリーズ No.245「副業者の就労に関する調査」 (2024-07): 6.0% of workers have a side job (women 7.4%, men 5.1%). — [JILPT No.245 page](https://www.jil.go.jp/institute/research/2024/245.html); [JILPT press PDF (2023-05-19)](https://www.jil.go.jp/press/documents/20230519.pdf)
- JILPT No.245, monthly income from all side jobs combined: the extract says "¥5万–10万未満" was the most common bracket at 30.0%, followed by "¥10万–15万" at 13.2%. A secondary summary says "under ¥5万" made up more than 40% (which only fits if the under-¥5万 range is split into finer brackets). Conflicting extracts; verify in the PDF. — [JILPT No.245 PDF](https://www.jil.go.jp/institute/research/2024/documents/0245.pdf); [生命保険文化センター summary](https://www.jili.or.jp/lifeplan/houseeconomy/9540.html)
- JILPT No.245, weekly side-job hours: 5–10 hours 27.6%, 10–20 hours 26.7%, under 5 hours 22.3%. About half work under 10 hours a week. — [JILPT No.245 PDF](https://www.jil.go.jp/institute/research/2024/documents/0245.pdf) (verify table location)
- Job総研 (2022, small sample): side-job and dual-job annual income averaged ¥192.6万 with a median of ¥100万. — [@IT/ITmedia](https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/articles/2208/16/news037.html)
- マイナビ (2022 column): people with one side job worked 8.1 hours a week on it on average; people with two or more worked 13.4 hours. — [マイナビ キャリアリサーチLab](https://career-research.mynavi.jp/column/20220210_22535/)

### Inferences
- Realistic baseline for the report: most side-job freelancers in Japan earn under ¥100万 a year from it, often under ¥10万. A typical side-job hourly rate is around ¥2,000 (パーソル median), not the ¥4,000–5,000 that engineer platforms advertise.
- The gap between the パーソル mean (¥3,617) and median (¥2,083) shows a right-skewed distribution. Averages quoted in marketing overstate typical outcomes.
- フリーランス白書 income figures (≈half at ¥400万 or more) describe mostly full-time, established freelancers: 48.5% work 140 hours a month or more. They are not a benchmark for 副業.
- Time input is small. Averages of 23 hours a month (パーソル) or under 10 hours a week (about half, JILPT) mean typical side-job income is limited by hours as well as by rate.

### Gaps
- No ランサーズ 2025 edition (survey early 2026) was found. The breakdown of the 2024 edition by the four worker types (people, economic scale, income) could not be extracted.
- The 内閣官房 2020 side-job income distribution was not extracted at bracket level (only "under ¥100万 is the most common bracket"). Results for the 令和4年度 survey were not retrieved. One extract mentioned "14.1% under ¥100万" without a clear denominator, so it is excluded.
- No government survey isolates software/IT side-job freelancers' income.

## 2. Hourly and monthly rates for 副業エンジニア (agency and platform data)

### Takeaway
Engineering rates published by agencies and platforms are far above the median for side jobs in general: about ¥4,400–4,500 an hour on SOKUDAN listings, and ¥74万–76万 a month for full-time freelance listings (フリーランススタート, 2025). These are listing averages for experienced engineers on mostly full-time contracts, not what side-job workers actually earn.

### Cited Findings
- SOKUDAN (副業/freelance matching): about 5,500 engineer listings from 2019-07 to 2024-12 average about ¥4,421 an hour, equal to ¥891万 a year or about ¥74万 a month. — [SOKUDAN Magazine](https://magazine.sokudan.work/post/us1w9Lfh)
- SOKUDAN, six engineer job types (front end, back end, infra, iOS/Android, ML, data science; listings 2019-06 to 2024-01): freelance engineer average ¥4,457 an hour. The same article gives the full-time employee IT engineer equivalent as ¥2,448 an hour (求人ボックス, 2024-04-22). — [SOKUDAN Magazine tips_89](https://magazine.sokudan.work/post/tips_89)
- エン・ジャパン「フリーランススタート」 monthly listing averages, all freelance listings, mostly full time: 2025-04 ¥74.6万; 2025-05 ¥73.8万; 2025-06 ¥73.9万; 2025-08 ¥76.3万. — [digitalpr 2025-04](https://digitalpr.jp/r/109695); [digitalpr 2025-05](https://digitalpr.jp/r/111728); [zaikei 2025-06](https://www.zaikei.co.jp/releases/3011677/); [zaikei 2025-08](https://www.zaikei.co.jp/releases/3097784/). Search extracts also gave a 2025-01 average of ¥74.9万, with consultants at ¥104.3万 and PMs at ¥86.7万; the URL of that release was not pinned down (possibly [zaikei 2817508](https://www.zaikei.co.jp/releases/2817508/)), so verify.
- レバテックフリーランス publishes a rate table by skill and uses "精算幅" (pay adjusts if monthly hours fall outside a set range). A commonly cited figure for its Tokyo-area web/app engineer listings (週5) is an average annual equivalent of ¥881万 (as of 2023-05). — [レバテック 単価一覧 PDF](https://levtech.jp/files/doc/freelance_cost.pdf); figure via [atsoho blog (secondary)](https://atsoho.com/blog/levtech-freelance-hyoban)
- Offers (overflow) surveyed 165 of its engineer and designer users: 80% had side-job experience. No hourly figure was given. — [CodeZine](https://codezine.jp/article/detail/13576)
- Comparison point for all side jobs: パーソル 2025 median ¥2,083 an hour, mean ¥3,617. — [パーソル総研](https://rc.persol-group.co.jp/news/release-20251028-1000-1/)
- A blog claims a median of ¥5,800 an hour (IQR ¥4,200–7,500) pooled across Offers, FLEXY, Findy Freelance and CloudWorks. The method and source are unclear; do not use as evidence. — [atsoho blog](https://atsoho.com/blog/programming-side-job-rate-2026)

### Inferences
- Agency figures skew high for structural reasons. They are (a) posted rates, not realized earnings; (b) mostly full-time 週5 contracts for engineers with several years of experience; (c) averages, pulled up by consultants and PMs (see the 2025-01 figures above, still to be verified); (d) from platforms that profit from attracting supply. The report should say so explicitly.
- A reasonable hedged range for an experienced engineer on a 副業 contract (週1–2日 or evenings) through these platforms is roughly ¥3,000–5,000 an hour. This is inferred from SOKUDAN listing averages and is not a measured distribution. Beginners on crowdsourcing will mostly earn less (see section 3).
- At the パーソル average of 23 hours a month, even ¥4,400 an hour comes to about ¥10万 a month gross, before platform fees and tax.

### Gaps
- No ITプロパートナーズ, Offers or クラウドテック published average side-job rate was found.
- No source gives a distribution (median or percentiles) of realized side-job engineer income, as opposed to listing averages.

## 3. Crowdsourcing platform fees, mechanics, and how many users actually earn

### Takeaway
The worker-side fees on the main Japanese platforms are high: ランサーズ takes a flat 16.5%; クラウドワークス takes 20% on the first ¥10万 of each contract (about 22% once 消費税 is added); ココナラ takes 22%. Payout fees come on top. None of the three listed companies publishes what share of registered users actually earn money. ココナラ's take rate is about 29% of gross transaction volume (GMV).

### Cited Findings
**ランサーズ**
- The worker (ランサー) fee is 16.5% (税込) of the contract amount, deducted when the work is completed. It applies to every format: project, competition, task, hourly, monthly and package. Clients pay a separate fee. — [ランサーズ FAQ](https://www.lancers.jp/faq/A1034/936)
- Since 2022-10-01 the worker fee has been a flat 16.5% instead of the earlier "up to 20%" sliding scale. — [ランサーズ notice](https://info.lancers.jp/28688)
- The official calculator deducts withholding tax (源泉徴収) from take-home pay for work subject to it. — [ランサーズ 報酬計算ツール](https://www.lancers.jp/tool/reward_calculation)
- Example from a secondary source: on a ¥10万 job the fee is ¥16,500 and take-home pay is ¥83,500. Clients pay 5.5%. — [atsoho blog (secondary)](https://atsoho.com/blog/lancers-system-fee-2026-comparison)

**クラウドワークス**
- The worker fee (ワーカーシステム利用料) is tiered by the portion of each contract amount: 20% on the part up to ¥10万, 10% on the part from ¥10万 to ¥20万, and 5% on the part above ¥20万. Task-format work pays a flat 20%. — [クラウドワークス 公式ガイド](https://crowdworks.jp/pages/guides/employee/fee); [CrowdWorks Academy (2026)](https://academy.crowdworks.jp/articles/crowdworks-tesuryo)
- 消費税 is added to the fee, so the effective deduction on small contracts is about 22%. — [CrowdWorks Academy](https://academy.crowdworks.jp/articles/crowdworks-tesuryo)
- FY2025/9 (Oct 2024–Sep 2025) consolidated results: revenue ¥22,657百万 (+32.4%), ordinary profit ¥1,759百万, net loss attributable to owners ¥257百万. — [決算短信 (JPX)](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/39000/081220251114502452_tse-acedjpsm-39000-20251114339000-ixbrl.htm)
- Registered users are cited as about 700万 workers and 100万 companies. The date of these figures is unverified. — [Monex scouter summary](https://scouter.monex.co.jp/news/detail/3900_20251114_act_20251114_153151_8)

**ココナラ**
- The seller fee was cut from 27.5% to a flat 22% (税込) for text services, video chat and paid blogs from 2021-04-12. Phone consultation was excluded. — [ココナラ news](https://coconala.com/news/532)
- The official help text says 22% is deducted when a transaction completes. Buyers pay 5.5% (税込). Seller blogs on the platform say video-chat services went back up to 27.5% from 2025-04-16 (verify). — [ココナラ seller guide](https://coconala.com/pages/guide_sell); [coconala blog (seller-written)](https://coconala.com/blogs/5267836/557278)
- Payout: a ¥160 transfer fee, waived for payouts of ¥3,000 or more. Earnings expire after 120 days and are then transferred automatically. Since 2025-01-27, payouts arrive the Thursday after the request. The official page may be dated, so verify. — [ココナラ seller guide](https://coconala.com/pages/guide_sell); [coconala blog](https://coconala.com/blogs/3205712/424712)
- FY2025/8: consolidated revenue ¥94.1億 (+42.8%). Marketplace GMV grew +4.6% year on year with a take rate of 29.1%. Registered members passed 550万. The GMV and take-rate figures come via the EDINETDB AI summary; verify in the IR deck. — [決算説明資料 (JPX)](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/41760/140120251014573204.pdf); [決算短信 (JPX)](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/41760/140120251014573235.pdf); [EDINETDB](https://edinetdb.jp/company/E36351/midterm-plans/)

**Upwork (overseas, brief)**
- Since about 2025-05-01 Upwork has charged freelancers a variable fee of 0–15% per contract, set before the proposal is submitted, replacing the old 20/10/5% tiers. Third-party estimates put the effective rate at about 10–13%. These come from third-party and competitor blogs, not Upwork's help center. — [GoLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026); [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-alternatives-in-2026-free-or-less-expensive-options-for-freelancers)

### Inferences
- Effective take-home on a ¥5万 job: ランサーズ about ¥41,750 (16.5%); クラウドワークス about ¥39,000 (20% plus tax, about 22%); ココナラ ¥39,000 (22%), before payout fees and income tax. These are calculated from the cited rates.
- ココナラ's 29.1% take rate roughly equals the 22% seller fee plus the 5.5% buyer fee plus other fees. About 3 yen in every 10 spent goes to the platform.
- Revenue growth (クラウドワークス +32%, ココナラ +43%) comes largely from businesses outside the marketplaces, such as agency and staffing operations or acquisitions (inferred; ココナラ GMV grew only +4.6%). It does not show strong growth in crowdsourcing payouts to individuals.

### Gaps
- None of the three platforms publishes the share of registered users with any earnings, or an earnings distribution (クラウドワークス showed top-earner examples in old FY2017 decks). This is a key gap: registered-user counts (700万, 550万) say nothing about how many people earn.
- ランサーズ payout fee and クラウドワークス payout and quick-payout fees were not confirmed from official pages. One extract mentioned クラウドワークス quick payout at 5.0% plus a transfer fee; unverified.
- クラウドワークス GMV for FY2025 and ココナラ GMV in yen were not extracted.

## 4. Demand for AI-related freelance work

### Takeaway
There is hard data on AI demand from Upwork (US-centric): GSV for AI-related work grew 60% year on year in 2024. Japanese platforms have published rankings and product launches but no comparable volume figures. In ランサーズ' 2025 ranking, the category "AI・機械学習・ChatGPT" fell from 1st to 10th, and work finishing or fixing AI output rose to the top.

### Cited Findings
- Upwork: gross services volume (GSV) for AI-related work grew 60% year on year in 2024. Upwork also estimated that 28% of US skilled knowledge workers work independently, with $1.5 trillion in earnings in 2024. This is US data. — [Upwork IR release (2025-04)](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-study-finds-1-4-us-skilled-knowledge-workers-now-work)
- Upwork full-year 2025 revenue was $787.8M (+2%). Guidance for 2026 is GSV +4–6%. No AI breakdown for 2025 was found. — [Stock Track/iTiger summary of Q4 results](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/1148531614)
- A secondhand claim says freelancers on AI-related projects earn 44% more per hour; no original Upwork source was found. — [CO/AI](https://m.getcoai.com/news/ai-skills-dominate-upworks-latest-research-report)
- ランサーズ「発注トレンドランキング2025」: 1st place was "デザインデータ修正・変換" (fixing and converting design data). Positions 1–9 all changed from the year before, and the previous 1st place, "AI・機械学習・ChatGPT", fell to 10th. ランサーズ describes 2025 as the year the "AI generates, humans finish" pattern took hold. — [CommercePick](https://www.commercepick.com/archives/80545); [MONOist](https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2510/17/news033.html)
- ランサーズ survey (2023-11): about 40% of freelancers use generative AI in their work, mainly for text generation, with higher use among people in their 30s–50s. — [@IT/ITmedia](https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/articles/2311/30/news060.html)
- ランサーズ 2024 survey lists "slow uptake of generative AI" (生成AI活用の遅れ) among freelancers as a problem. — [@IT/ITmedia](https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/articles/2503/26/news082.html)
- クラウドワークス: pre-registrations for its AI-work matching service "AIクラウドワークス" passed 10,000 (press release 2026-07-28), with official launch and acceptance of business requests planned for 2026-08-20. No order-volume growth figure was given. The URL could not be opened to confirm the extract. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000319.000050142.html)
- ギークス survey of IT freelancers (2025-09): whether AI is used has become an important factor in choosing a project. — [@IT/ITmedia](https://atmarkit.itmedia.co.jp/ait/spv/2509/30/news047.html)

### Inferences
- In Japan, the signs of AI demand point to work that AI changes (editing, checking and finishing AI output; AI-assisted development) rather than a large pool of "build an AI model" jobs for side-job workers. The fall of the AI/ML category to 10th in ランサーズ' ranking is consistent with this.
- The Upwork 60% figure is the only hard volume number found. It describes a mostly US marketplace in 2024 and should not be presented as Japanese demand.

### Gaps
- No Japanese platform published a count or growth rate for 生成AI 案件 (job postings or GMV).
- How AI-related jobs pay on Japanese crowdsourcing platforms was not found. Many entry-level "AI" tasks on JP platforms (e.g., AI writing) are anecdotally low-paid, but no citable data was found.

## 5. Legal framework: フリーランス新法, 下請法/取適法, contract basics

### Takeaway
The フリーランス・事業者間取引適正化等法 (in force 2024-11-01) requires clients to state contract terms in writing or electronically and to pay within 60 days of delivery. For contracts of one month or longer it bans withholding acceptance of work, cutting pay, below-cost pricing (買いたたき) and similar practices. Enforcement in its first full year (FY2025) produced 1,552 actions, almost all of them guidance (指導) rather than formal recommendations. 情報通信業 was the industry with the most cases, and late or missing payment was the most common violation.

### Cited Findings
- Scope: the law covers 業務委託 (outsourced work) to a "freelancer", meaning a business with no employees. 公取委 and 中小企業庁 handle fair trading; 厚労省 handles working conditions. — [経産省/公取委 資料](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/kyoso_seisaku/20250909003.pdf); [厚労省 PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001101551.pdf)
- Duty to state terms: on commissioning work, the client must immediately state the work content, pay amount, payment date and both parties' names, in writing or electronically. — [経産省/公取委 資料](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/kyoso_seisaku/20250909003.pdf); [一橋大学 対応案内](https://www.hit-u.ac.jp/zaimu/zuikei/free_taiouannnai.pdf)
- 60-day rule: pay must be made within 60 days of receiving the deliverable or service, and as soon as possible within that. If no payment date is set, the delivery date counts as the payment date. — [経産省/公取委 資料](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/kyoso_seisaku/20250909003.pdf); [幻冬舎GOLD (secondary)](https://gentosha-go.com/articles/-/79940?page=3)
- Banned acts for contracts of one month or longer: refusing to accept work, cutting pay, returning work, 買いたたき, forcing purchases or use of services, demanding unjust economic benefits, and unjust changes or redo requests. — [経産省/公取委 資料](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/kyoso_seisaku/20250909003.pdf)
- FY2025 (令和7年度) enforcement, announced 2026-06-10: 1,626 new cases opened, of which 604 came from complaints; 1,597 cases processed; 1,552 actions taken (10 勧告, 1,542 指導). 情報通信業 was the top industry with 575 cases, ahead of professional and technical services (326) and transport (135). Main violations: not paying by the due date 1,135; failure to state terms 1,126; 買いたたき 250. Payment plus stating terms make up about 80% of 2,727 violation counts. Kanto region 845 (54.4%). — [公取委 報道発表 2026-06-10](https://www.jftc.go.jp/houdou/pressrelease/2026/jun/260610_FL.html); [LOGISTICS TODAY](https://www.logi-today.com/962882)
- Example of a 勧告: in 2026-09 公取委 issued a 勧告 to 日本郵便 for failing to state terms to 167 freelancers, 140 of whom had no payment date set and were not paid by the legal deadline. — [Data Max](https://data-max.co.jp/article/86747)
- Before the law took effect, government survey data showed about 40% of freelancers had experienced trouble such as non-payment, late payment or no purchase order (secondhand citation). — [幻冬舎GOLD](https://gentosha-go.com/articles/-/79940)
- 下請法 relevance: from 2026-01 下請法 was overhauled into the 中小受託取引適正化法 (取適法). It adds an employee-count test alongside the capital test. Where both laws could apply, a freelancer with no employees is protected mainly by フリーランス法. Practitioners advise meeting the stricter standard (60-day cash payment, written orders). — [マネーフォワード クラウド契約](https://biz.moneyforward.com/contract/basic/25023/); [弥生](https://www.yayoi-kk.co.jp/kigyo/oyakudachi/freelance-law02/)
- An amended 公益通報者保護法 adding freelancers as protected whistleblowers is reported to take effect 2026-12 (practitioner source; verify with 消費者庁). — [マネーフォワード クラウド契約](https://biz.moneyforward.com/contract/basic/25023/)
- Help line: 厚労省 runs "フリーランス・トラブル110番" for free consultation. — [厚労省 PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11901000/001144663.pdf)
- フリーランス白書2026 includes a section on relations with clients after the law took effect. No percentages could be extracted. — [フリーランス協会](https://blog.freelance-jp.org/20260609-25645/)

### Inferences
- For a side-job developer, the useful protections are: insist on written terms (scope, price, payment date); the 60-day payment limit; and, for work of one month or longer, protection against one-sided price cuts and unpaid redo requests. Late payment and missing terms are exactly the most common violations, and IT is the industry with the most enforcement cases, so this risk is real for software work.
- Enforcement is mostly 指導 (1,542 of 1,552), so the law corrects behavior rather than securing back pay. Keeping evidence such as messages, specs and delivery records is still essential.

### Gaps
- Working-condition duties under the law were not verified from a cited extract in this session. From background knowledge (verify with 厚労省): accurate job-ad wording, harassment-prevention measures, consideration for childcare and nursing care, and 30 days' notice of termination for contracts of six months or longer.
- Whether and how the law applies to transactions on crowdsourcing platforms (where the platform is an intermediary) was not confirmed from an official Q&A.
- No percentages on trouble after the law took effect were extracted from フリーランス白書2026.

## 6. Risks: unpaid work, side-job scams, employer rules, overwork

### Takeaway
Consultations about "info products" (情報商材) sold as side-job money-makers are falling: 6,256 in FY2023, 4,163 in FY2024 and 2,623 in FY2025. Broader "内職・副業その他" consultations are still about 11,000–12,000 a year, and SNS is a common entry point. About 64% of companies now allow side jobs, so about a third still do not; check the 就業規則. Overwork is a measurable risk: about 30% of side-job workers exceed 45 hours a month of overtime plus side work.

### Cited Findings
- 国民生活センター, consultations about 情報商材: 2023年度 6,256; 2024年度 4,163; 2025年度 2,623 (as of 2026-05-31, excluding referred cases). The count has fallen since the FY2018 peak of about 8,700. — [国民生活センター 情報商材](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/soudan_topics/data/infoproducts.html)
- "内職・副業その他": 2023年度 11,192 (15th among product/service categories), up from 8,538 in FY2022. — [2023年度 消費生活相談の状況](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/pdf/n-20240807_3.pdf). The FY2025 release extract shows "内職・副業その他" at 16th with 11,751 cases, 37.9% via online or remote sales. The year column was unclear; verify. — [2025年度 消費生活相談の状況 (2026-08-05)](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/pdf/n-20260805_1.pdf)
- All consultations in FY2025: 1,006,377 (100.6万), up from 91.3万 in FY2024. The average contract or purchase amount was ¥88万 and the average amount already paid ¥50万. These cover all consultations, not side jobs only. — [国民生活センター 2025年度](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/news/data/n-20260805_1.html)
- 国民生活センター 2023 study of hard-to-resolve cases involving 起業・副業 (starting a business or side job) trouble: SNS was frequently involved. The extract mentions N=15,454 for FY2021. — [国民生活センター 調査報告書 (2023-03)](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/pdf/n-20230322_2_2.pdf)
- 国民生活センター warning: schemes advertising "副業サポート" or investment target young people and push them into borrowing from several lenders to pay support fees. — [国民生活センター 若者向け注意喚起 No.2 (2021-06)](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/pdf/n-20210603_1.pdf)
- SNS-related consultations of all kinds rose from 3,143 in FY2010 to 19,251 in FY2019 (to end of February), about six times. — [国民生活センター (2020-04-09)](https://www.kokusen.go.jp/news/data/n-20200409_1.html)
- 消費者庁 warning about "もうけ話" (money-making pitches for investment and side jobs) that start on SNS. — [消費者庁](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/consumer_policy/caution/caution_036/)
- Unpaid work: in FY2025 enforcement of フリーランス法, "not paying by the due date" was the most common violation (1,135 counts), and 情報通信業 led all industries (575 cases). — [公取委 2026-06-10](https://www.jftc.go.jp/houdou/pressrelease/2026/jun/260610_FL.html)
- Employer rules: 64.3% of companies allow employees to have side jobs (2025), up from 50.9% in 2018 and 60.9% in 2023. 29% accept side-job workers from other companies. — [パーソル総研 第四回 (2025-10-28)](https://rc.persol-group.co.jp/news/release-20251028-1000-1/); [パーソル総研 第三回 (2023)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000023.000111116.html)
- Overwork: about 30% of side-job workers exceed 45 hours a month of main-job overtime plus side-job hours, and about half of those have not reported their side job to their employer. — [パーソル総研 第四回 PDF](https://rc.persol-group.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/thinktank/data/sidejob4.pdf)
- マイナビニュース (2026-04) advises checking the 就業規則 before starting a side job. — [マイナビニュース](https://news.mynavi.jp/article/20260425-4384779/)

### Inferences
- The scam risk for someone selling real skills (development) is mostly on the buyer side: paying for courses, info products or "support" that promise side-job income. On the seller side the main risks are non-payment and scope creep, the most common フリーランス法 violations.
- Falling 情報商材 counts do not mean the risk is gone. The steady 11,000+ "内職・副業" consultations suggest the schemes have moved into other formats such as SNS, task scams and support contracts (inference; category boundaries are not documented here).
- About 36% of companies still do not allow side jobs (100% − 64.3%). Unreported side work interacts with working-hours rules, so employees should check rules and declare the work.

### Gaps
- No 2025-specific count of SNS-originated side-job scams was found. 消費者白書 age-group data was not retrieved.
- No average loss per case was found specifically for side-job or info-product cases (only the all-consultation averages above).
- No data was found on non-payment rates specifically on crowdsourcing platforms, where escrow (仮払い) exists, compared with direct contracts.
